# Visual Epic 134A implementation receipt

Date: 2026-07-28

## Scope

Epic 134A introduces the explicit wgpu upload boundary in
`C:\Users\rsasa\Workspace\SpatialRust\crates\spatialrust-render-wgpu`.

- A caller supplies an existing `Arc<WgpuRuntime>` to `WgpuRenderer`.
- `upload` accepts borrowed point, line, or triangle contracts and is the only
  host-to-device crossing in this slice.
- SoA point positions are packed during the named upload operation. Optional RGB
  and scalar attributes and triangle indices receive separate device buffers and
  receipt events.
- `GpuGeometry` records topology, counts, logical buffer bytes, and the exact
  renderer residency.
- `GpuGeometry::recycle` returns buffers to a bounded 32-buffer pool. Recycling
  through another renderer fails before changing either pool.

No render pass, screenshot readback, implicit synchronization, device selection,
or CPU/GPU auto-placement is introduced by this slice.

The three-test suite ran against the selected headless wgpu adapter and proves:

- interleaved XYZ order is stable and incomplete triples are rejected;
- two XYZ/RGB/scalar points produce three named events and exactly 40 uploaded
  logical bytes;
- one indexed triangle produces two named events and exactly 48 uploaded logical
  bytes;
- recycling retains three buffers and the next equal upload consumes them;
- geometry cannot be recycled through a distinct renderer sharing the same
  underlying wgpu runtime.
